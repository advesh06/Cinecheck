Academic Integrity & Responsible AI Safeguards

CineCheck is developed for the Text & Web/Social Media Analytics (TWSMA) curriculum. The system analyzes theatrical releases scheduled between 1 January 2025 and 30 September 2026. A classification of "Potentially Suspicious" is a probabilistic linguistic indicator and must never be treated as legal or definitive proof of deception without independent human review.
