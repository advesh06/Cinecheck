Academic Methodology & TWSMA Syllabus Alignment

Architectural breakdown, syllabus unit mapping, and responsible AI governance for CineCheck

Curriculum Mapping (TWSMA Units)
Module 1.1
Foundations of Text Analytics

Implements tokenization, lowercasing, normalization of elongated letters ('sooooo' → 'soo'), excessive punctuation compression, URL removal, negation preservation ('not', 'never'), and TF-IDF unigram & bigram vectorization.

Module 2.1
NLP & ML Classification Pipelines

End-to-end scikit-learn ML pipeline training Logistic Regression and Multinomial Naive Bayes. Automatically conducts held-out stratified validation and model serialization (joblib).

Module 2.3
Bias Mitigation & Error Analysis

Evaluates error disparities and performance parity across star rating sub-cohorts (low ratings vs high ratings) and movie genres to prevent systematic penalty against critical human reviews.

Module 6.1
Explainability & Interpretability

Decomposes model predictions into linear TF-IDF feature weight contributions (positive suspicious pulls vs negative genuine pulls). Integrates stylistic heuristics and pairwise cosine similarity clustering.

Module 6.2
Ethics, Privacy & Safeguards

Upholds user data protection, isolates ground-truth from model inferences, prevents defamatory accusations by using responsible categories ("Potentially Suspicious"), and mandates human moderation.

Module 6.3
Monitoring & Versioning

Maintains model artifact metadata, version timestamps, reproducible random seeds, and persistent SQLite analysis audit histories.

Responsible AI Safeguards & Ethical Notice
1. Probabilistic Categorization

Reviews are categorized as "Potentially Suspicious" or "More Likely Genuine". CineCheck strictly prohibits asserting that any review is "definitely fake" based solely on statistical model output.

2. Protection Against False Accusations

Genuinely enthusiastic fans frequently write short, superlative reviews with exclamation marks. Stylistic indicators alone do not prove malicious intent or commercial paid astroturfing.

3. Ground-Truth Data Isolation

When raw unlabelled datasets are uploaded, the system never fabricates ground-truth labels. Model inferences are stored in distinct database fields (predicted_label, suspicion_score).

4. Synthetic Simulation Transparency

In adherence to BookMyShow platform Terms of Service, private user account profiles are not harvested. Demonstration training reviews are transparently simulated benchmark samples.
