Academic Integrity Notice on Synthetic Benchmark Performance

In strict compliance with academic standards: all metrics above are computed exclusively on the held-out test split of the curated benchmark dataset (cinecheck_reviews.csv). High benchmark accuracy demonstrates effective mathematical separation of the learned synthetic and stylistic distributions. However, performance on live, unlabelled, adversarial real-world platforms cannot be conclusively proven from synthetic benchmark splits alone.
